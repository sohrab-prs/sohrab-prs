# Sohrab Seyyedi Parsa

**Geospatial Analytics | Data Science | AI-Assisted Spatial Analysis**

I am an interdisciplinary researcher and practitioner with an MSc in Data Science from Sapienza University of Rome (2026), an MSc in Urban Planning from Tehran University of Art, and professional experience in GIS, spatial analysis, spatial modelling, geospatial data management, and urban and regional planning.

My recent MSc Data Science thesis investigated a stateful, code-first LLM-agent framework for geospatial analysis, focusing on tool-augmented workflows, supervisory reasoning, validation, benchmarking, and reliable computational execution. A research manuscript is in preparation; the thesis repository remains private pending further review and release decisions.
